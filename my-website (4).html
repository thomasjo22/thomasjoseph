About Me


Thomas Joseph is a young Egyptian novelist studying psychology at the British University in Egypt and London South Bank University. His writing centers on the human psyche and its inner conflicts, blending literary style with psychological insight to craft contemporary narratives.

His debut novel, "The Crime of the Thanaweya Amma" (جريمة الثانوية العامة), marks his first work of fiction with Dar El Shorouk — one of Egypt's most prestigious publishing houses.

My Latest Book

جريمة الثانوية العامة

Have you ever imagined walking into an exam terrified of failing it, only to find yourself entangled in a murder instead? That's exactly what happens to Zein. On the very first day of his final high school exams, he becomes the prime suspect in the murder of a fellow student.

Alone and cornered by accusation, Zein refuses to give up. He knows that if he wants to survive, he has to solve the mystery himself. But the closer he gets to the truth, the more he discovers that the school he thought he knew is riddled with dark secrets.

Behind its veil of crime and mystery, the novel offers a precise dissection of the human psyche when stripped bare by fear. It's a journey that proves the most important lesson of your final year isn't found in a high exam score, it's discovering the monster that can live inside the people closest to you. That is the real boogeyman.
